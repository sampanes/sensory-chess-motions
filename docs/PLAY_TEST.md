# Play-test sheet

Every milestone in `ROADMAP.md` ends with the same open item: watch a real kid play. This is the
list of what to watch for, so ten minutes with a kid answers as many questions as possible.
Adult play does not count. Say nothing, do not point, and write down what happens.

Get ready: the grown-up panel (hold the gear on the home screen for 2 seconds) has "Start over from
the beginning" for a truly fresh start, and "Open every friend and puzzle" to jump ahead. Turn sound
on. Any screen can be opened directly with its link (the list is in `README.md`).

## A kid who has never seen it (fresh start)

| Watch | It works if |
|-------|-------------|
| Home screen: do they tap the bouncing friends folder, then the king? | They get to the king without help. |
| Meeting the king: he hops around, then a hand taps him. | They tap him. If they tap during the hops and give up, the hops are too long. |
| First puzzle, the lesson: only the king and a berry; a hand taps the king. | They tap the king, see the spots appear, and tap the berry. Note if they tap the berry first (fine) or tap the hand itself. |
| After the win, a hand taps the green button. | They press it and go on to puzzle 2 alone. |
| Puzzles 2 to 5: no lesson any more, the hint hand only after a quiet spell. | They finish without the hint, or use the hint and then carry on. |
| A puzzle that can no longer be finished rewinds by itself. | They try again without getting upset. |

## Each friend (one or two puzzles is enough)

| Watch | It works if |
|-------|-------------|
| A new friend wakes up in the folder and waves. | They notice and open it. |
| The first puzzle of the rook, bishop, queen, knight and pawn worlds. | They finish it without an adult showing how that friend moves (this is the milestone 2 bar). |
| Two friends at once (the last world, and Saturday's daily puzzle). | They tap the other friend to switch. |

## The other bubbles

| Watch | It works if |
|-------|-------------|
| Tiptoe, first set: do they understand why the guard woke up? | After one or two wakes they start going around the watched squares. |
| Tiptoe, second set (shoo): puzzle 1 has a guard in the only lane. | They hop onto the guard, by themselves or after the hint. |
| Tiptoe, third set (stay safe): puzzle 1 offers a capture that wakes the bishop. | After the wake they go for the bishop first. If they repeat the same capture three times, the lesson is not landing. |
| Pawn walk: a race against the gray pawn, and the angry queen when it gets home first. | They laugh or at least are not scared, and try again. |
| Bedtime: one checkmate puzzle. | A 4 to 5 year old finishes one without help (the milestone 4 bar). |
| Today's puzzle. | They can finish it; note the friend and the day if not. |

## The big touchscreen

| Watch | It works if |
|-------|-------------|
| Does the game pick the big-screen look by itself? | The board fills the height and the buttons sit beside it. If not, set "Big screen" in the grown-up panel and note the screen size. |
| Reaching the corners (home, start over) and the green button. | They can reach them standing or sitting where they play. |
| Edge swipes, a resting arm, two kids touching at once. | Nothing leaves the game or gets stuck. Try "Full screen" in the grown-up panel if the browser's own bars get tapped. |

## The lab (for the grown-up who decides what graduates)

| Watch | It works if |
|-------|-------------|
| Crossing (`#/lab`): spots, steps or plain. | Pick the variant where the kid stops trying to put the bishop on the wrong-color home. Do the footprints mean anything to them? |
| Set up the board (`#/lab/setup`): ghosts or copy. | With ghosts: is it too easy to be a puzzle? With copy: do they look up at the sleeping side? Level 4 (queen and king) is the real question. |
| The reveal when the whole side stands (levels 5 to 7). | They notice the board turned to wood and say something about it. Then put a real set next to it. |

## For the grown-up, afterwards

- Open "What they know" in the grown-up panel: does it match what you just watched? A friend the
  kid clearly knows should say "Knows it" after five puzzles finished alone.
- Print the card (`?card`) and try it beside a real set: can the kid name the friend for each piece?
- If anything looked unreadable, try the high-contrast board.

Write the answers into `ROADMAP.md` as a dated status note under the milestone they belong to.
